How Red Wing works
  • Place a bet from before takeoff. You can use two bet panels at once.
  • The multiplier climbs from 1.00x. Press Cash out before the plane flies away to win your bet times the multiplier.
  • If the plane flies away first, that bet is lost.
  • Auto tab: turn on Auto bet to repeat your bet every round, and Auto cash out to collect automatically at a multiplier you choose.
  • A bet placed during a flight starts in the next round.
  • Each crash point is drawn before takeoff. Many rounds end early and a few go very high. Balance has no cash value.
